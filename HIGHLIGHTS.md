# Highlights

The most noticeable changes per release, a few lines each. Every detail and
every fix is in the [full changelog](https://github.com/yakuda-stack/Dream-VoiceTraining/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## 1.1.7 — 2026-09-16

- **Highlights button** next to *Changelog* under *Settings → Info* — this page.
- **Changelog and Highlights moved into the links box**, as a second row under
  Source, Discord and Ko-fi.
- **New Discord invite link** in the app and the README.
- **Source code sorted into `core/`, `voice/` and `ui/`**, plus a version
  script for releases.

## 1.1.6 — 2026-09-10

- **Fixed dB scale for the detail spectrogram**, so two recordings can be
  compared side by side.
- **F1, F2 and F3 orientation lines** across the spectrogram.
- **Project links are buttons in a row**, each with an icon and the colour of
  its service on hover.

## 1.1.5 — 2026-09-09

- **Folder names built from parts** — calendar week, week span, date, free text.
- **The detail spectrogram stays sharp when zooming in**, down to individual
  glottal pulses.

## 1.1.4 — 2026-09-09

- **Tutorial screenshots are found in more install locations.**

## 1.1.3 — 2026-09-09

- **Spectrogram in the detail window.**
- **Import existing recordings** (WAV, 8/16/24/32-bit).
- **Your own recording types** and **selectable practice texts**.
- **Pitch history reads out under the mouse.**

## 1.1.2 — 2026-09-07

- **Options tab for naming recordings** — file names from parts, subfolders
  per day, week, month or year, running counter.
- **Changelog button** under *Settings → Info*.

## 1.1.1 — 2026-09-05

- **Choose where recordings go**, and move existing ones along.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-09-04

- **Input picker next to the microphone box.**

## 1.0.9

- **The downloaded EXE offers to install itself** on first start.

## 1.0.8

- **Project links on the last page of the introduction.**

## 1.0.6 — 2026-09-03

- **Settings open on the Info tab**, which replaces the About dialog.

## 1.0.5

## 1.0.4

## 1.0.3

## 1.0.2 — 2026-09-02

## 1.0.1 — 2026-09-02

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-01

First release.

- **Live view** with pitch, formants, weight and level, a scrolling
  spectrogram and target zones.
- **Recordings and sessions** with six recording types and a sortable list.
- **Detail view** with eighteen metrics, target profiles and comparison.
- **English and German interface**, AUR package, AppImage.
